Recommended pair

Issue Stamp + The Cover. Both are extracted directly from the site's existing masthead grammar (the № glyph, hairline rule, Fraunces, status dot, rust accent) — the whole stack reads as one publication, not a product with branding bolted on.

Drop-Cap D and Press Proof are stronger visually but introduce ideas (italic monogram, gold foil) the site doesn't already commit to — that contradicts its under-promising voice.
